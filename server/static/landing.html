The Core Challenge

Failed payments are not all the same.

When recurring subscriptions or checkout transactions decline, typical systems respond with naive periodic retries or aggressive heuristic spam. In practice, unconstrained interventions introduce compounding operational and commercial friction.

⚠️

Customer Fatigue & Churn

Repeatedly pinging customers with uncoordinated notifications on non-recoverable failures creates friction and accelerates customer churn.

🚫

Gateway & Processor Penalties

Blind retries on hard card declines trigger issuer rate limits, elevated clearing fees, and merchant processing account penalties.

🔒

Fraud & Dispute Exposure

Indiscriminately retrying stolen instruments or velocity-flagged transactions directly increases fraudulent settlement liabilities and dispute risks.

⏱️

Context & Timing Blindness

Static lookup rules fail to distinguish between month-end salary delays, transient bank maintenance, and genuine authentication drops.

💡

The REVIVE Philosophy

"The goal is not to intervene on every failure. The goal is to recover revenue selectively while keeping automation governed."

End-to-End Workflow

From failed payment to governed recovery

Every failed transaction moves through a deterministic, observable lifecycle where candidate actions are rigorously scored, verified against hard safety invariants, and executed under strict authorization.

01

Failed Payment

Captures decline webhook or cart drop-off event with error code, gateway telemetry, and invoice metadata.

02

Context Analysis

Extracts customer tenure, historical reliability, checkout funnel stage, and payment method characteristics.

03

Failure Diagnosis

Classifies root cause into actionable taxonomies (Transient, Insufficient Funds, Drop-off, Permanent Decline).

04

Recoverability & Action Scoring

Computes calibrated recoverability probabilities and ranks candidate Expected Value (EV) against intervention costs.

05

Policy & Safety Gates

Evaluates deterministic rule hierarchy (attempt caps, fatigue limits, fraud blocker, confidence threshold).

06

ExecutionAuthorization

Issues a single-use, bounded authorization token strictly when all deterministic policy gates pass.

07

Controlled Execution Simulator

Dispatches permitted action (Smart Retry, Payment Link, Reminder, Human Escalation) with live state verification.

08

Recovery Outcome

Observes simulated settlement, measures recovered revenue, and calculates incremental uplift (ΔR).

09

Audit Event

Persists immutable telemetry with diagnostic reasoning, policy checklist results, and execution timestamps.

🛡️

Critical Security Invariant: Recommendations do not directly execute.

The decision intelligence layer possesses zero direct execution authority. Automated execution requires a validated ExecutionAuthorization issued only after the policy and safety gates pass.

ZERO DIRECT EXECUTION AUTHORITY
Contextual Reasoning

Context-aware recovery decisions

REVIVE uses deterministic, interpretable decision logic to produce contextual recovery recommendations across complex payment scenarios.

TAXONOMY ENGINE

Root-Cause Categorization

Differentiates between technical bank network timeouts, user-induced authentication cancellations, seasonal liquidity delays, and hard expired cards.

UNCERTAINTY CALIBRATION

Diagnostic Confidence Scoring

Quantifies diagnostic certainty on a 0.0 to 1.0 scale. Any recommendation with confidence < 0.85 fails closed to human review.

ECONOMIC OPTIMIZATION

Expected Value (EV) Ranking

Calculates Expected Value: EV = P(Success) × Amount − Cost(Action) − Penalty(Churn) to select optimal interventions.

FUNNEL TELEMETRY

Checkout Funnel Awareness

Detects exact abandonment stage (Cart vs Payment Form vs OTP stage) to trigger high-intent reminders rather than intrusive retries.

TEMPORAL SCHEDULING

Liquidity & Salary Timing

Identifies balance exhaustion declines and schedules automated retries during predictable liquidity windows (e.g. 1st or 30th of the month).

ACCOUNT HISTORY

Customer Reliability Profile

Weighs past successful transaction tenure, recurring mandate compliance, and prior dispute history before authorizing proactive nudges.

Deterministic Policy Engine

Automation with guardrails

"Fail closed when the system should not act." When policy conditions are not satisfied, the system refuses automated execution and routes the case to safety queues.

P004 CONFIDENCE GATE

Low-Confidence Escalation

Blocks automated action whenever diagnostic certainty falls below 85% (5.64% of benchmark opportunities routed to human review).

P005 FRAUD BLOCKER

High-Risk Account Gate

Stolen card flags, velocity abuse, and suspicious IP telemetry are 100% blocked from automated action and routed to risk review (0 leaks).

P003 ATTEMPT CEILING

Bounded 2-Attempt Limit

Enforces a hard ceiling of at most 2 automated recovery attempts per invoice to prevent runaway retry loops and fees.

P007 FATIGUE GUARD

Customer Contact Limits

Caps communication touches at 2 contacts per billing cycle to eliminate spam and preserve merchant brand reputation.

P008 COOLDOWN GUARD

Mandatory 300s Cooldown

Enforces a 5-minute cooldown interval between repeated attempts on the same transaction to respect banking queues.

P002 STATE CHECK

Double-Recovery Prevention

Re-checks live payment state immediately prior to dispatch to prevent executing on already resolved or captured payments.

A measurable tradeoff, rigorously verified.

REVIVE is evaluated against passive observation (NO_ACTION), blind retries (NAIVE_RETRY), and unconstrained heuristics (RULE_BASED) across 50,000 synthetic holdout transactions over 5 independent seeds. All model thresholds were frozen exclusively on seed 42 before holdout evaluation.

⚖️ Primary Architectural Tradeoff
"REVIVE recovered 75.19% of RULE_BASED gross revenue while using 22.37% fewer interventions."
REVIVE deliberately trades some gross recovery for fewer interventions and explicit safety governance. Unconstrained RULE_BASED heuristics achieve higher gross recovery through aggressive, repetitive retry attempts without attempt limits, cooldowns, or fraud risk checks. REVIVE enforces hard 2-attempt limits (P003), customer communication fatigue limits (P007), 300s cooldowns (P008), diagnostic confidence boundaries (P004), and blocks all automated actions on high-risk accounts (P005).

  • 2. Real customer behavioral dynamics or psychographic responses.
  • 3. Real-world chargeback prevention or bank-level dispute guarantees.

  • 6. Production-scale throughput or distributed network reliability.

Scientific Integrity

What REVIVE is — and isn't

To maintain transparency and credibility, we clearly document the operational boundaries of this prototype.

✓ What REVIVE Is

  • ● Simulation-based research prototype: Designed to evaluate governed autonomous decision-making on failed payments.
  • ● Deterministic & interpretable decision engine: Context-aware reasoning with explicit mathematical Expected Value ranking.
  • ● Zero-trust policy governance: Hard deterministic safeguards enforcing attempt caps, cooldowns, and fraud blocks.
  • ● Reproducible benchmark framework: Scientifically tested across 50,000 holdout transactions across 5 seeds.

ℹ️ What REVIVE Isn't

  • ● Not a live payment processor: Does not connect to live banking networks or charge real credit cards.
  • ● No real customer data: Uses strictly synthetic transactions, generated personas, and simulated behavior.
  • ● No ungrounded safety claims: Does not claim zero risk, guaranteed recovery, or complete chargeback prevention.
  • ● Not an LLM-per-transaction runner: The core decision and policy engine operates deterministically.
